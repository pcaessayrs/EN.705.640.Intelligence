In an animal brain, a [[lobe]] is a collection of relatively-interconnected neurons. ([[@mousleyTopologicalTurningPoints2025|Mousley et al., 2025]]) Different lobes in have different functions and those functions are, in [[neurotypical]] people, common between different people.

#### The mediotemporal lobe
The lobe primarily responsible for [[memory]][[@parisiContinualLifelongLearning2019|(Parisi et al., 2019)]], particularly the hippocampus.
#### The temporal lobe
The lobe primarily responsible for [[audition]], [[language]], and [[emotion]]. Notable, the amygdala is the cortex in the temporaral lobes primarily responsible for [[emotion]] [[@schullerAgeArtificialEmotional2018|(Schuller & Schuller, 2018)]] recognition and processing.
#### The occipital lobe
The lobe primarily responsible for [[vision]][[@livingstoneSegregationFormColor1988|(Livingstone & Hubel, 1988)]].
