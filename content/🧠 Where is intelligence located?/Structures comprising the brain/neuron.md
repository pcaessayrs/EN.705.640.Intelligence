_A single agent of information that can be dynamically configured to interact with other such agents to elicit information when activated._

In the [[The animal brain|animal brain]], the neuron is a nerve cell.

In an [[artificial neural network]], the neuron is (usually) a data structure consisting of a node of a (usually) directed acyclic graph with associated weights consisting of floating points that describe the neuron's connections to other neurons (or to a final output value).
