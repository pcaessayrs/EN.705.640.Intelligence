In animals, the hippocampus in the medial temporal lobes is responsible for the formation of long-term memory through complementary learning systems.[[@mcclellandExtendingMachineLanguage2020|(McClelland et al., 2020)]] This lobe is critical for new learning, and damage to it can prevent the acquisition of new skills without impacting already-learned skills.
