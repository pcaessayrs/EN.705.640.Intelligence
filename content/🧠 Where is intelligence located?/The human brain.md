_2. Any similar specialized structure responsible for intelligence, regardless of the [[🧠 What is the substance of intelligence?]]._

In animals, the hippocampus in the medial temporal [[lobe]]s is responsible for the formation of long-term memory through complementary learning systems.[[@mcclellandExtendingMachineLanguage2020|(McClelland et al., 2020)]] This lobe is critical for new learning, and damage to it can prevent the acquisition of new skills without impacting already-learned skills.

#### The mediotemporal lobe
The lobe primarily responsible for [[memory]][[@parisiContinualLifelongLearning2019|(Parisi et al., 2019)]], particularly the hippocampus.
#### The temporal lobe
The lobe primarily responsible for [[audition]], [[language]], and [[emotion]]. Notable, the amygdala is the cortex in the temporaral lobes primarily responsible for [[emotion]] [[@schullerAgeArtificialEmotional2018|(Schuller & Schuller, 2018)]] recognition and processing.
#### The occipital lobe
The lobe primarily responsible for [[vision]][[@livingstoneSegregationFormColor1988|(Livingstone & Hubel, 1988)]].
