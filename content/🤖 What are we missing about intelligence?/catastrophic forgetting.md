_The failure mode in [[artificial neural network]]s where new knowledge interferes with previously learned knowledge. [[@parisiContinualLifelongLearning2019|(Parisi 2019)]]_

Unlike ANNs, human brains do not forget what they've learned as a function of learning more; instead, the [[lobe|hippocampus]] seems to be capable of adding increasingly many factoids and memories all throughout life. By contrast, [[Aging and the brain|aging]] leads to differences in recall as connections between memory structures change in the brain.
