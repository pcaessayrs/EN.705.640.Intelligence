[[👶 The newborn brain|Newborns]] are remarkable for their ability to learn purely by being exposed to new inputs without, or in addition to, explicit instruction as to the mapping between inputs and meaning (i.e. unsupervised and/or reinforcement learning). [[@dupouxCognitiveScienceEra2018|Dupoux, 2018]] describes this process as cognitive bootstrapping.

![[infant learning.png]]
_Stages of infant language learning (Dupoux, 2018)._

It is expected that the structure of the human brain enables [[Intelligence is rapid|rapid]] learning, tuning humans towards developing similar structures in the brain to ingest [[information]] in a way that's relatively consistent between people, leading to people with analogous structure and functioning in their brains being classified as "neurotypical".
