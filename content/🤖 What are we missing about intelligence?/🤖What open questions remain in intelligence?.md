### How is memory preserved in the long-term?
In primates, the hippocampus in [[The human brain|the animal brain]] is responsible for [[memory]]. Contemporary AI can suffer from [[catastrophic forgetting]] or from quadratic (or worse) growth in parameters needed to avoid forgetting.

### What defines consciousness?
[[@hildtArtificialIntelligenceDoes2019|(Hildt, 2019)]] provides several suggestions:
- We could identify consciousness in terms of self-refential and globally available knowledge.
- We could focus on "access consciousness."
- We could leave this as an open question on purpose.
### What is the effect of [[embodied cognition]]?
Contemporary AI is typically a disembodied [[artificial neural network]]. Would AI be more effective as an embodied being?
### How do we create artificial [[emotion]]al intelligence?
Emotion itself is not as well understood as [[audition]] or [[vision]]. Is it possible to create systems that recognize, or even feel, emotions?
#### How do we make AI that is safe? And how do we safely make AI?
AI has several structural issues, ranging from extreme environmental impacts to [[@gebruOxfordHandbookAI2019|automation of bias]] to the impact on personhood to the effects of AI on end-users.
It may be useful to debate whether any particular AI should, or should not be, [[Turing complete]].
