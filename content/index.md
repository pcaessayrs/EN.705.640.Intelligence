title: "intelligence: rapid, fluid, generalizable, durable learning"
---
**Intelligence is [[rapid]], [[fluid]], [[generalizable]], [[durable]] [[learning]].**

A system is intelligent even before it [[learning|learns]]. a human [[newborn]] has substantial capacity for [[rapid]] learning from birth.

Contemporary [[artificial intelligence]] is often [[fluid]] and [[generalizable]], but the learning is rarely [[durable]] and not even a fraction as [[rapid]] as a [[newborn]] when normalized for the amount of [[information]].

**The structure of a system determines its intelligence: what and how it learns.**
