newborns are often characterized by their effective [[intuition]] or "startup software" that allows a newborn to learn from [[unsupervised data]] [[@lakeBuildingMachinesThat2016|(Lake 2016)]] despite a [[paucity of stimulus]], a process called #bootstrapping.

[[@dupouxCognitiveScienceEra2018 |Dupoux (2018)]] asserts that newborns learn through unsupervised or weakly supervised learning that progresses through a [[curriculum learning]].
