_General intelligence: Intelligence capable of generating solutions._

Learning is generalizable when a variety of [[input]]s map transformatively to a variety of [[output]]s. generalized [[content/index]] can apply to new inputs and create new outputs using some mixture of new information and old information. Generalized learning comes as a _process for problem solving_, not _the solution for particular problems._
