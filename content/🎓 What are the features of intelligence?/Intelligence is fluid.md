_Fluid intelligence: Intelligence capable of adaption and change._

*For intelligent liquids, see [[The slime mold brain]].

Intelligence is _fluid_ if it can adapt to new domains of [[information]]. Typically, this leads to the [[🧠 What is the substance of intelligence?|physical medium governing intelligence]] being reallocated to parse the new inputs and solve the new problems.
