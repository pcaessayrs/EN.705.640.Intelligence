_The capacity for processing sounds, including localization and interpretation._

Unlike [[vision]], audition is a temporal data stream, so localization of sound (identifying)
Humans process audition in the [[lobe|temporal lobe]] after registering the input through the [[cochlea]].

In addition to the complicated structures of the inner ear, humans have pinnae, outer ears, which work to reshape sound inputs before processing and which exhibit a diversity of shapes. The relationship between the physical realities of the input organs (ears) and the workings of the brain are important to understanding [[embodied cognition]].
