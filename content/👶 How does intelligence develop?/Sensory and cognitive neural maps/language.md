_The conveyance of meaning by the mapping of concepts to words._

Infants are understood to learn language through a series of increasingly sophisticated high-level feature capturing, starting from basic phonemes and progressing into words and then sentences [[@dupouxCognitiveScienceEra2018|(Dupoux, 2018)]]. Trained humans seem to understand the rules of their language despite a [[poverty of stimulus]] about the total rules of their language.

Depending on the available modalities, language can be transmitted and sensed through a combination of audition, vision, and tactition. 

In humans, language is processed in multiple [[lobe|lobe]]s of the brain.
