_The capacity for [[learning]] and retaining that learning over a period of time or interactions._

In humans, the [[lobe|hippocampus in the medio-temporal lobe]] is primarily responsible for adding new memories. (_However, there is also additional long-term learning possible through other methods, such as "muscle memory".)

[[catastrophic forgetting]] is the failure to retain memory outside of a fixed context window (e.g. forgetting caused by gaining more [[information]]).
