_newborn (noun): an infant, a newly born human child._

Newborns are often characterized by their effective intuition or "[[startup software]]" that allows a newborn to learn from [[unsupervised data]] [[@lakeBuildingMachinesThat2016|(Lake 2016)]] despite a [[poverty of stimulus]], a process called #bootstrapping.

[[@dupouxCognitiveScienceEra2018 |Dupoux (2018)]] asserts that newborns learn through unsupervised or weakly supervised learning that progresses through a [[curriculum learning]].
